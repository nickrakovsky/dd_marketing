Suppose a supervisor tells me an unload took three times as long as a regular one. The crew had to shift the freight inside the trailer before they could pull it out. Sitting in the office, I can picture putting a strap on it, shifting it over and bringing it straight onto the dock. I’d allow some extra time for that. I’d still be wondering where the rest of the time went.

The higher up you go in operations, the less time you get on the floor. When a load ties up a door, you’re often piecing together what happened by talking to the people who handled it. In this case the supervisor has told me exactly what held them up. The part I’ve supplied myself is how easy it should have been to shift that load.

Imagine we’ve booked an hour at 10:00 to receive a steel frame. Three pallets for another warehouse sit by the trailer doors because that delivery was due first. Once the truck is on the road, the other warehouse moves its appointment to noon. Our crew leaves at 11:30, so we agree to take the truck at 08:00 with all three pallets still aboard.

The moment I’d have missed is when the two pallets by the doors come off. Now the forks can reach the frame. Look along the path its far end would take, though, and you can see where the arm would catch the third pallet. I’d counted on a straight pull from there.

At one facility we had <SmartLink href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_mPNqKLqel8" title="Fix Warehouse Exceptions Before They Back Up the Dock" snippet="DataDocks · 2026" anchorText="a compact forklift that could get into tight spaces" />. On a difficult unload, one of the first things we’d look at was whether we could use it to draw material within reach. We could do that with freight the little forklift couldn’t lift. It mattered which part of the job you were asking it to do.

If I’m up in the office thinking a strap would have saved the crew all that time, I’d want to stand at the trailer doors with the operator. Where would the far end go if we pulled straight back? Once I can see why the move I had in mind won’t work, I have a different question for the person who got it out. What would have made this unload easier?
