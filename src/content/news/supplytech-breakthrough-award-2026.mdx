DataDocks received the **Smart Warehousing Innovation of the Year** award in the 2026 SupplyTech Breakthrough Awards. The program announced its winners on June 11, 2026; DataDocks shared the news in a company announcement on June 18.

The award sits within the program's warehousing category. SupplyTech Breakthrough evaluates technology across supply chain and logistics, with categories spanning areas such as inventory, transportation, visibility and material handling.

## Connecting dock scheduling and yard management

DataDocks helps facilities coordinate appointments and the work around them. Carriers book through a shared portal, while warehouse teams manage schedules and follow the progress of loads. The company's original announcement describes how appointment information connects arrival, yard staging, dock assignment and departure.

For an operations team, those events provide the detail needed to understand a delay: when a truck arrived, when its status changed and when it departed. DataDocks records these changes with timestamps and the user responsible, giving the team a common record to review.

## Turning activity into useful information

The platform's operational reporting covers throughput, carrier punctuality and time spent at the facility. Teams can use those records to identify recurring delays and review their scheduling practices.
