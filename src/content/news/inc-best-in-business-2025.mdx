DataDocks was named to **Inc.'s 2025 Best in Business list**, with recognition in the **Best in Bootstrapped** and **Best in Innovation** categories. The company announced the news on December 4, 2025.

Inc.'s company profile lists DataDocks as a 2025 Best in Business honoree in software. DataDocks' original announcement identifies the two recognition categories and describes the company's approach to developing its dock scheduling and yard management platform.

## Built around feedback from operations teams

In the announcement, CEO Nick Rakovsky explained how conversations with people working on the loading dock inform the product. Scheduling requests, double bookings and drivers waiting in the yard are practical problems that shape what the team builds.

That approach connects directly to the everyday work supported by DataDocks. Facilities can set the information required for a load, let carriers book their own appointments and keep arrival and departure records in one place. The aim is to give coordinators more time to manage the operation and improve its processes.

## A shared view of the schedule

Carrier self-service is part of that workflow. A common booking system gives carriers and facility teams access to appointment information without having to coordinate every booking through separate messages.
