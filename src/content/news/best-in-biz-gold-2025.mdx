DataDocks earned **gold** in the **Technology Solution of the Year – Supply Chain and Logistics** category at the 2025 Best in Biz Awards. The official product winners list names DataDocks as the gold recipient in this category.

The program announced its winners on December 3, 2025. DataDocks published its company announcement on December 10, marking the recognition in the program's 15th year.

## Recognition for supply chain technology

Best in Biz Awards uses judging panels drawn from the media, including editors and reporters. The gold award places DataDocks within a category dedicated to technology for supply chain and logistics.

DataDocks serves warehouses and production facilities with software for dock scheduling and yard management. Its role is to bring appointment booking, facility activity and operational records into a shared workflow.

## From booking to an operational record

A booked appointment is the starting point. Once a load arrives, the team can record its progress through the facility and its departure. Timestamped changes create a record that people can consult when investigating a delay or reviewing carrier performance.

Operational dashboards bring those records together to show throughput, punctuality and duration. Teams can also connect scheduling information to their existing business systems through API or EDI integrations.
