DataDocks founder Nick Rakovsky joined The New Warehouse for episode 414, published on August 28, 2023. The conversation explores his experience managing warehouses and the communication and accountability problems that led him to start DataDocks.

## A closer look at dock scheduling

The episode covers how customers and carriers book appointments, how booking rules help validate information, and how shared updates can reduce the need for phone calls and emails. It also considers the role of visibility in helping warehouse teams coordinate their work.

Those themes offer a useful starting point for reviewing an appointment process. Where does a carrier find the current instructions? Who knows when a delivery changes? How does the warehouse team receive that information before the truck arrives? Clear answers make it easier to spot where a process depends on someone passing along a message manually.
