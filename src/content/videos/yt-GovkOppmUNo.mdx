## Your pickers aren't lazy: 70% of their shift is wasted walking and searching for inventory.

Are slow order pickers destroying your warehouse productivity? A Georgia Tech study revealed that order pickers spend 55% of their time just walking to items and another 15% searching for misplaced inventory right in front of them. That means 70% of picking labor is completely wasted due to broken warehouse processes, poor receiving accuracy, and unoptimized pick paths.

Fixing your order picking speed requires optimizing your warehouse layout. Moving high-velocity SKUs closer to shipping doors and creating clear pick paths instantly cuts travel time. Additionally, ensuring 100% receiving accuracy prevents pickers from wasting time searching for inventory that isn't actually there. Addressing these two operational flaws yields an immediate 20% to 30% improvement in picking speed and shipping throughput.
