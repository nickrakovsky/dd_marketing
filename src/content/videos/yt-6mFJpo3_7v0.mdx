## You're paying detention fees for fast loads because your check-in paperwork is broken.

Why are you getting hit with carrier detention and demurrage fees when your warehouse team unloads trucks in under 30 minutes? The silent killer of loading dock efficiency isn't physical labor—it's broken check-in paperwork and poor driver communication. If a truck driver checks in without clear instructions, missing documentation, or no idea which dock door to back into, your facility racks up detention charges while the truck sits idle.

Eliminating demurrage costs requires a streamlined driver check-in and checkout flow. Automated text messages and real-time carrier notifications keep drivers informed throughout the loading and unloading process, ensuring paperwork is signed and ready before the truck ever leaves the dock. Fixing driver communication eliminates waiting time, prevents carrier disputes, and keeps your inbound logistics running smoothly.

This video covers detention and demurrage fees, carrier check-in procedures, driver communication automation, loading dock paperwork bottlenecks, inbound freight management, dock coordinator workflows, and reducing carrier charges.
