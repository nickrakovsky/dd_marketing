## Drivers showing up with half-written PO numbers on scrap paper is wasting hours on your dock.

Inbound truck check-in delays are a constant headache for warehouse coordinators. Drivers frequently arrive at the guard shack or loading dock with incomplete PO numbers scribbled on paper, leading to frantic phone calls between dispatchers, sales teams, and customers just to figure out what load is being dropped off. This manual search process wastes 20 to 40 minutes per truck, causing massive loading dock traffic jams.

Solving truck check-in confusion requires dynamic dock scheduling software with flexible PO lookup tools. Systems that allow partial number searching across customer POs, internal POs, or bill of lading numbers instantly locate the correct appointment in seconds. Streamlining inbound freight check-in saves your team hours of administrative back-and-forth and keeps your dock schedule on track.
