## Warehouse Receiving Process: Stop Costly Errors Early

If your warehouse receiving process lets bad counts, damaged product, or missing paperwork get past the dock, you’re probably going to deal with that same problem again later...when it’s harder and more expensive to fix.

A receiving mistake doesn’t always look like a big deal when the truck is sitting at the door and your team is trying to get it unloaded. But once that mistake moves into inventory, putaway, picking, or shipping, more people have to spend time fixing it.

In this video, we break down the 1-10-100 Rule and practical ways to catch receiving problems earlier instead of letting them move downstream through your warehouse operations.

You’ll learn:

✅ Why catching an error during receiving is easier than fixing the same problem after product has already moved through the warehouse
✅ How a simple dock-floor layout can reduce wasted forklift travel and unnecessary movement
✅ How reviewing your warehouse receiving process can expose extra steps, waiting, and other slowdowns your team may not notice during a normal shift
✅ How “blind loads” can test whether your receiving team is actually ready for damaged goods, incorrect counts, or missing paperwork
