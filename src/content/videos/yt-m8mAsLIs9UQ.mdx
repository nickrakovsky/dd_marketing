## Your floor guys know the problem before you do.

Are you tired of playing "broken telephone" between your warehouse floor and your front office? The people on the distribution center floor know exactly what is happening—they know when a pallet is broken, when incoming freight is missing, or when unloading a truck is taking too long. But if you lack supply chain visibility, that critical information never makes it to the people who need it.

When warehouse communication breaks down, it causes massive logistics operations bottlenecks. Your sales team doesn't know a load is damaged. Your accounting department misses out on charging accessorial fees because they didn't know about the delay. And the freight carrier or customer ends up getting the wrong information. Relying on manual updates puts massive stress on the warehouse manager and destroys your warehouse efficiency.

To fix this, you need automated warehouse reporting. Implementing a system that provides instant visibility means that the moment a load is flagged on the floor, the right people in your organization are notified instantly. Stop letting logistics data fall through the cracks. Learn how to improve warehouse communication, streamline distribution center reporting, connect your warehouse floor to your sales and accounting teams, and build a logistics operations strategy that eliminates human error.

This video covers warehouse management tips, logistics reporting, supply chain visibility, inbound freight management, distribution center communication, warehouse operations strategy, WMS reporting, dealing with damaged freight, and how to improve logistics customer service.
