## Telling your warehouse team to 'get to it when they can' guarantees it never gets done.

Why do warehouse cleaning tasks and staging maintenance constantly get ignored? Telling your logistics team to clean an area "when they have time" ensures it will never be prioritized. Without clear role expectations, explicit metrics for success, and firm completion dates, floor staff will continually push non-urgent tasks aside.

Fixing warehouse accountability requires setting concrete goals and hard deadlines. Clearly defining what success looks like for each role boosts team morale by giving workers measurable benchmarks. Using specific target dates—such as "this staging area must be cleared by Friday at 3 PM"—eliminates vague excuses, establishes clear operational priorities, and improves overall facility organization.
