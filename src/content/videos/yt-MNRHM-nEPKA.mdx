## Running your warehouse on legacy WMS reports is giving you complete fake data.

Is your warehouse data actually accurate? One of the biggest mistakes a new warehouse manager can make is blindly relying on legacy WMS, ERP, and accounting reports set up years ago. Standard reports built by previous employees often pull outdated metrics that do not reflect current distribution center operations, leading to flawed decision-making and hidden operational bottlenecks.

Before using warehouse metrics as your golden standard, you must audit how those reports are generated. Working directly with accounting and operations teams ensures your data reflects true inventory levels, dock turnaround times, and labor efficiency. Designing custom warehouse reports tailored to your specific facility goals prevents false assumptions and improves supply chain decision-making.
