## Why your best warehouse workers are leaving for $2 an hour

Are you struggling with high warehouse turnover? The real reason your best warehouse workers are leaving for a competitor down the street for a few extra dollars an hour isn't just about the money. It's because you are accidentally trapping them in their roles. The biggest mistake a warehouse manager can make is taking a highly efficient logistics employee—a "well-oiled machine"—and leaving them in the exact same spot just because it makes the operations management easier.

If you want to improve employee retention in your distribution center, you have to stop punishing your best staff for being good at their jobs. When logistics workers hit a ceiling and realize they have no room for growth, they get frustrated and quit. To fix your supply chain bottlenecks and build a strong logistics team, you need to implement cross-training on the warehouse floor. Give your key personnel opportunities to learn new warehouse operations, understand inbound freight management, and move up the ladder.

If you were recently promoted to warehouse manager, use your knowledge of the floor to identify your strongest players and build a career path for them. Train your logistics supervisors to identify talent so your supply chain operations run smoothly. Learn how to manage a warehouse team, reduce distribution center turnover, improve logistics communication, and build a warehouse operations strategy that actually retains top talent.
