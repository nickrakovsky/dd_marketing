## Tracking every single pick per hour is the fastest way to ruin your warehouse culture.

Are your warehouse KPIs destroying your team dynamic? It is incredibly easy to micro-manage your floor staff by tracking every single pick per hour and putting together strict standard operating procedures (SOPs). However, forcing your logistics team to hit hyper-specific hourly numbers creates massive workplace anxiety, pressure, and resentment. When some warehouse workers experience natural daily fluctuations or feel like others aren't pulling their weight, forcing strict hourly metrics destroys warehouse culture and team morale.

Instead of tracking every single physical step along the way, operations managers should shift their perspective to daily outcomes. Focus on total daily picks, reducing mispicks, maintaining quality control staging, and ensuring everything is put away perfectly at the end of the shift. Shifting your logistics management style to daily outcomes removes anxiety, builds a better warehouse culture, and improves overall facility productivity.
