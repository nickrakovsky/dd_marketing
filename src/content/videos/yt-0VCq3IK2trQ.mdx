## Asking your warehouse team for feedback and then letting it slide hurts morale more than you think.

Are your pre-shift meetings and daily standups actually hurting your warehouse morale? Asking your logistics team about their pain points and floor problems during Monday morning meetings is a great way to start, but if you let those ideas slide, you destroy workplace trust. Warehouse managers have a million equally important supply chain issues to deal with every day, making it easy to forget employee feedback. But coming back months later without addressing their operational complaints ruins your team culture.

The ultimate warehouse management hack for tracking employee feedback is using a simple whiteboard in the breakroom or staging area. Scribbling down their operational complaints, floor ideas, and equipment problems right in front of them proves you are listening. Keeping that whiteboard visible allows the entire shift to track progress against those goals and see how the team is performing.
