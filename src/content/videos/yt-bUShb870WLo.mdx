## Carriers win detention arguments because your driver checkout process takes 30 minutes.

Are you constantly fighting with freight carriers over detention and demurrage charges at your facility? Even if your warehouse team finishes loading or unloading a truck well within the time limit, poor checkout procedures ruin your defense. Drivers sitting in their cabs for 30 minutes waiting for signed bills of lading (BOL) or clearance to leave rack up detention time that falls squarely on your facility.

Preventing unfair carrier detention claims requires automating your driver checkout process. Automated digital paperwork, instant SMS notifications, and clear dock coordinator handoffs ensure drivers know the exact second their load is finished. Eliminating physical bottlenecks during checkout protects your business from unnecessary carrier fees and endless administrative disputes.

This video covers carrier detention disputes, driver checkout procedures, automated bill of lading sign-offs, demurrage charge reduction, loading dock management, freight carrier communication, and distribution center efficiency.
