## Nobody tracks truck turnaround time, and it's the easiest way to prove your dock's value.

Are you looking for an easy operational win in your warehouse management? Tracking truck turnaround times for inbound and outbound freight is one of the most overlooked metrics in supply chain management. Most small facilities and major competitors never measure how long a truck sits at the loading dock, leaving massive warehouse efficiency gains on the table. Comparing turnaround times across shifts, weeks, and years immediately reveals operational bottlenecks and staffing gaps.

Showing your upper management that you reduced turnaround times from an hour and a half down to an hour and fifteen minutes gives you concrete data to justify promotions, raises, and labor budgets. Faster turnaround times mean higher dock throughput, cleaner staging areas, less carrier congestion, and better overall distribution center flow.
