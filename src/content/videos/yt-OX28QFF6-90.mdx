## Why constantly criticizing your warehouse team makes them slower

Are you struggling with warehouse staff accountability and low team morale? The real reason your warehouse team has zero ownership is because you are falling into a common logistics management trap. When a newly promoted warehouse manager walks the distribution center floor, it is easy to only focus on the negative: people ignoring safety procedures, logistics workers moving slowly, and staging areas that need to be cleaned up.

If you want to improve warehouse efficiency, you have to stop harping on your logistics team. Constantly pointing out the bad things happening in your supply chain operations will actually make your warehouse workers slower and less efficient. To get true ownership from your floor staff, you need to implement positive reinforcement. A simple shout-out during a weekly warehouse meeting or calling out a specific inbound freight department before a shift goes a long way in operations management.

When your logistics team feels heard and appreciated, they will naturally fix warehouse bottlenecks, improve truck turnaround times, and follow loading dock procedures.
