## You would never lose a forklift, but losing a trailer for a month happens all the time.

Are you losing track of trailers and shipping containers in your yard? You would never lose a forklift for two days because your team would notice immediately, but losing an entire container or trailer for a month or two is a massive problem in big distribution centers. Failing to track yard inventory results in high demurrage fees, detentions charges, and terrible carrier management. When a customer calls and asks about their freight, telling them "we'll see if your trailer is still here" destroys your logistics reputation.

You need full yard visibility to know what is on your facility at any given time. Proper yard management means knowing which trailers are loaded, which are empty, and which are ready for pickup. Improving your yard flow and tracking inventory reduces warehouse chaos, cuts operational waste, and improves shipping and receiving communication.

This video covers yard management software, tracking trailers, container tracking, warehouse logistics bottlenecks, shipping and receiving, 3PL yard visibility, inventory tracking mistakes, distribution center organization, and holding carriers accountable. Stop letting trailers sit lost in your yard for months.
