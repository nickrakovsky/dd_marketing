## Your supervisor calling in sick is the perfect opportunity to test your next team lead.

How do you find future leaders within your warehouse operations? Every distribution center has a select few floor workers who want to move up the ladder and get promoted to team lead or warehouse supervisor positions. The biggest problem new managers face is giving these employees leadership experience when they have none. The secret to unlocking employee potential and training future warehouse supervisors is strategic cross-training and task delegation.

When a team lead or supervisor calls in sick, it creates the perfect opportunity to give an up-and-coming employee a day or two of management responsibility. Let them see what it is like to manage the loading dock schedule, handle carrier communication, and organize the warehouse shift. You can also delegate abandoned or low-priority projects to these workers to test their problem-solving skills. Cross-training your best floor workers prevents employee turnover and gives them a completely new perspective on your supply chain operations.
