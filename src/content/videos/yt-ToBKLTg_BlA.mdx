## Carrier GPS tracking is almost useless for facilities

Are you tired of freight carriers lying about their ETA? While GPS tracking and telematics are great for your own fleet, GPS tracking is completely useless when dealing with third-party logistics (3PL) carriers. When a warehouse manager relies on outside carriers, they lose supply chain visibility. You have no idea if a truck is actually going to show up on time, if they are stuck in traffic, or if they are going to be a complete carrier no-show.

If you want to improve warehouse management, you have to stop letting trucks show up to your loading dock whenever they want. When you lack inbound freight visibility, it ruins your dock scheduling and creates massive warehouse overtime. The only way to fix this logistics operations bottleneck is to take control of what you can track: the exact moment they arrive at your facility.

By using automated dock scheduling software like DataDocks, you can automatically track carrier no-shows and build carrier scorecards. Tracking logistics KPIs allows you to hold third-party carriers and customers accountable for missing their time slots. Stop relying on broken telematics and start using hard data to manage your warehouse operations. Learn how to track inbound freight, handle 3PL blind spots, improve loading dock efficiency, and build a warehouse operations strategy that forces carrier compliance.
