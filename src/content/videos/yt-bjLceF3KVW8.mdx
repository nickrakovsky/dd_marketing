## To the front office, your warehouse is just a bunch of numbers.

Are you constantly fighting with your sales and accounting teams over logistics operations? The biggest problem in supply chain management is the massive disconnect between the front office and the distribution center floor. To the sales and accounting departments, your warehouse operations are just a bunch of numbers coming in and out of a system. They have no idea how physical goods actually move, which leads to impossible customer promises, supply chain bottlenecks, and massive stress for your warehouse team.

If you want to improve warehouse efficiency and fix this interdepartmental friction, you need to give the front office a reality check. Invite your support, sales, and accounting staff down to the loading dock to join your warehouse shift meetings. When corporate employees actually see the physical difficulties your logistics staff deals with day-to-day—like broken pallets, carrier no-shows, and inbound freight delays—they stop treating the warehouse like a spreadsheet.

Bridging the gap between the front office and the warehouse floor is the ultimate operations management hack. Learn how to manage a warehouse team, improve logistics communication, run effective shift meetings, align your sales and supply chain departments, and build a warehouse operations strategy that actually works. Stop letting the front office ruin your warehouse efficiency.
