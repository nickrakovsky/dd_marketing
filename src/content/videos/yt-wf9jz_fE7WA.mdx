## 8 Brutal Truths Every Warehouse Operations Manager Needs to Hear

These warehouse management tips are the things I wish somebody had told me before I became responsible for the whole operation.

When you move into a warehouse manager role, the job changes fast. Leadership wants results. Supervisors need answers. The floor has problems that can’t always wait. And somehow you’re expected to keep all of it moving without being everywhere at once.

In this video, I break down eight lessons I learned the hard way about managing people, making decisions, and keeping warehouse operations under control when the pressure starts piling up.

You’ll learn:

✅ Why being the person between leadership and the floor can feel like you’re getting pulled from both directions
✅ How to build a good relationship with your team without making expectations and accountability unclear
✅ Why warehouse managers sometimes have to make a call before they have every piece of information
✅ How vague standards create arguments later because everybody leaves with a different idea of what “good” looks like
✅ Why small wins matter when you’re taking over an operation and everything can’t be fixed at once
✅ How clearer boundaries with supervisors and employees can keep every problem from landing back on your desk

Success in a warehouse isn't about having all the answers on day one. It’s about small wins, clear boundaries, and avoiding the "career killers" that most managers never recover from. Use these 8 truths to take control of your career and slingshot your facility forward.
