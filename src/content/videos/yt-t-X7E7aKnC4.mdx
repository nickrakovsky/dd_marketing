## Without a floor mechanic doing quick safe fixes, your rental machine costs will skyrocket.

Facilities that maintain low equipment downtime rely heavily on skilled floor mechanics capable of executing fast, safe repairs. When electrical issues or hydraulic hose failures happen mid-shift, waiting hours for off-site service technicians halts warehouse operations, increases expensive equipment rentals, and forces premature machinery replacements.

Having an on-site mechanic who can implement immediate, safe "band-aid" fixes allows your team to finish the shift before scheduling dedicated downtime for complete maintenance. Quick-response internal repairs reduce reliance on temporary equipment rentals, prevent compounding maintenance issues, and keep warehouse productivity high.

This video covers warehouse equipment repairs, on-site facility mechanics, reducing forklift downtime, temporary equipment rental costs, material handling maintenance, and shift continuity strategies.
