## Stop Letting Carrier Emails Run Your Dock

That quick email from a carrier asking to book or change a dock appointment might seem harmless.

But when your entire dock scheduling process is through email, a simple change can turn into hours of back-and-forth, missing information, unexpected freight, detention fees, paperwork problems, and another fire your warehouse team has to put out.

And the time spent answering emails is only part of the problem.

In this video, we break down why email-based dock scheduling creates so much hidden work throughout your operation...and how to build a more standardized system where carriers, coordinators, supervisors, and your floor team can all see the information they need.

You’ll learn:

- Why a seemingly minor carrier email can create problems 
- The hidden communication gaps that throw your team off
- Why email threads struggle to keep warehouse exceptions standardized
- How centralized dock information can give everyone  better visibility
- A smarter approach to dock appointment scheduling 
- And much more!
