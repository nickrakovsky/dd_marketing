Use
Plan a scoped warehouse review, record evidence and carry findings through to action and verification.
Scope limits
The 28 starter checks need the requirements, goods, activities and operating conditions of your review. They do not provide a complete legal or sector-specific audit, certification or an overall pass score.

Examples are fictional and separate from blank working records.

Use the pack

Start with the decision
Record the question this review must answer, the areas to examine and who will use the findings. You can plan evidence before making observations.
Establish the criteria
Name the applicable procedure, agreement or other requirement and its version. Assign it to the relevant check. Write 'Not yet established' where it is unknown.
Keep the sample visible
Record the goods, locations, shifts, period and transactions examined. Keep exclusions and evidence limits with the conclusion.
Use evidence references
Enter short observations and record references. Use roles and site aliases where sufficient; avoid copying customer details or sensitive records into the pack.
Leave uncertainty open
A blank status means Not reviewed. Record missing criteria, conflicting accounts and unanswered questions. Label a possible cause as a hypothesis.
Assign the next step
Give each concern an action owner, target date and verification plan. Keep the person doing the work separate from anyone who must approve resources or make a decision.
Separate completion from effectiveness
Record what was completed, then what the follow-up check showed. Work completed without effectiveness evidence remains awaiting verification.
Adapt the working record
Save a copy before entering your review. Keep the starter check IDs and add local checks where needed. The examples illustrate recording; they are not findings about your operation.

Status meanings

StatusMeaning
Not reviewedNo assessment recorded. Plan the evidence still needed.
Evidence supports the stated checkYour evidence supports the check within the stated scope. Name the criterion and supporting reference; missing support remains a gap.
Concern foundRecord the observation, requirement and evidence, then assign follow-up. Do not assume the cause is established.
Inconclusive or conflicting evidenceThe available information does not settle the question. Preserve the uncertainty and request the next evidence.
Not applicable — reason recordedExplain why the check falls outside this review. A missing reason remains an unresolved scope gap.

Working fields

Peach cells
Editable fields. Cream cells contain starter prompts, definitions or calculated context. Warnings use darker orange text. Save a review copy before entering evidence.
Check question
Select a check question to open its full starter prompts. Each prompt has a return link to the register.
Scope choice
Selected, Not selected or blank. Blank means the review scope has not yet been assigned. Keep excluded entries rather than deleting them.
Criterion reference
Enter the requirement or reference ID and version for this check. No requirement is assigned to all checks automatically.
Blank status
Not reviewed. It never counts as evidence supporting a check.
Finding and action IDs
Use the same stable IDs across the finding, action and verification tables. One finding may have several actions. Keep a single verification row per action; retain past verification history in evidence references.
Verification result
Not yet checked, Effective, Not effective or Inconclusive. Work completed is recorded separately. A result needs a reviewer, date and evidence reference.
Extra checks and rows
Use the blank custom rows or extend the relevant Excel table. Preserve the starter IDs. Give new checks and actions unique IDs. When extending a table, retain its validation and calculated columns.
