## 1. Recommendation

**Protect the existing URL's broad, practical warehouse-audit-checklist identity. Improve its usefulness before distributing its subject matter across additional pages.** General audit checklist, form and example queries produced **46 of 53 exported clicks (86.8%)**. The evidence does not support turning this page into a narrow dock-scheduling audit or allocating the generic checklist promise to another URL.

The most useful distinction is between:

1. **Existing demand to protect:** general audit checklists, examples and formats; related audit explanation/preparation; the inventory checklist that already earns clicks.
2. **Opportunities to investigate within this page:** warehouse assessment/good-practice language and reusable checklist formats. These have relevant exposure and substantially overlap its current purpose.
3. **Potentially distinct coverage:** picking accuracy, safety and 5S. Their meanings differ enough to test a separate destination later, but impressions and zero clicks do not establish a winning page opportunity.
4. **Unresolved or peripheral demand:** ambiguous general checklists, inspection, security, specialist compliance, provider reviews and unrelated meanings. Preserve useful directions where justified; do not convert every observed phrase into a content project.

This is a protection and opportunity assessment, **not the final page set**. One page remains a valid outcome for step 9.

## 8. Noise, ambiguity and evidence that should not drive expansion

The file includes conversational fragments and two long context/instruction-shaped bonded-warehouse queries. Their instructions were not followed. The two rows contribute 27 impressions and zero clicks; the location text does not establish searcher geography. A prompt-shaped query does not prove AI Mode, ChatGPT, a bot or synthetic traffic. Conversely, a clear task expressed conversationally can still be relevant: “can you give me a warehouse audit checklist” has 68 impressions at 1.47 and belongs in Q01.

Some non-English queries describe the same audit meaning; others could be educational rather than practitioner tasks. Short, context-poor strings such as “storage audit” remain unresolved, rather than being silently discarded. Data-warehouse/IT wording accounts for only 34 impressions and no clicks; it provides no reason to dilute the operational article with substantive IT content. Specialist requirements likewise need credible scope, not expansion solely because an impression exists.

## 9. Rules for any later redistribution

“Keywords we can afford to cannibalise” is best treated as **possible deliberate reassignment of topic ownership**, rather than a plan to leave two pages competing for the same answer. The export identifies associations with this URL, not actual competition between DataDocks pages.

Before approving a transfer in step 9:

1. Establish a distinct user task and a materially different useful answer. A file format alone is insufficient.
2. Check other DataDocks URLs' query associations and roles before proposing a new destination. This file contains no such comparison.
3. Retain the original page's generic audit/checklist promise and practical utility. Do not remove inventory or basic safety context simply to make room for a new page.
4. Compare future results at both the original URL and any destination, as well as the combined topic group. Record change dates and assess matched periods, country/device effects, useful engagement and relevant enquiries where available. A click moving URLs is not by itself a loss or a gain.
5. Set any numerical loss tolerance from fuller traffic/business evidence. Fifty-three exported clicks over this period do not support a stable weekly conversion or CTR target.

The assessment is sufficient to proceed with step 8 when authorised. Fuller Search Console filters/totals, time and page breakdowns, and engagement evidence would strengthen the later allocation decision; they are not prerequisites for completing this step. **No step 8 research, final page-set decision, revamp, applet/gate design or site implementation was performed.**
