Verified 15 September 2026. Research and draft copy, not a published page.

## Date policy

Store the award issuer's announcement date separately from DataDocks' original press-release date and the new site's eventual publication/update date. For an archive of existing DataDocks announcements, use the original company-release date on cards, explicitly label it "Originally announced", and explain any later page publication in metadata. Do not silently backdate a newly written article.
