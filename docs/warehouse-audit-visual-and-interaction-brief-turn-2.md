## Reader purpose

The public page helps a warehouse team choose what to review, examine evidence and assign useful follow-up. It also helps a director commission a review and understand what a finding means for a decision. The checklist is the immediately usable answer; the method and examples help the reader apply it. The reusable files help carry that work into a team review.

The opening must explain these uses through recognisable concerns: repeated stock differences, late dispatches, customer evidence requests and a provider renewal. Do not open with an abstract taxonomy of stock counts, daily checks and new-site setup. The brief's ambiguity analysis determines coverage and onward directions; it is not a list of distinctions every reader needs to decode at arrival.

Move the single complete Excel/PDF pack offer directly after the opening paragraph. Before any disclosure, show its benefit, the optional DataDocks email-subscription exchange and explicit coming-soon/unavailable status. Preserve anchor `warehouse-audit-working-pack` at this early section and put its contents-navigation entry first. Restore the two-line, full-width cream native disclosure with an outlined circular chevron: title “Excel and PDF audit pack”, action “Preview pack contents and signup”. It opens the approved artwork, full contents and the sole inactive accepted signup prototype in place. The contents include editable evidence/action records, printable floor sheets, completed examples and a management review template. Keep the complete offer self-contained, with conditional future-availability wording. Remove the former late offer; this is not a separate file peek directing readers to search for another offer.
