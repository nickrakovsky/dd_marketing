When receiving cannot match an arriving load to the correct purchase order, the next job may be a phone call or an inbox search. Someone must find the reference before the team can complete the required check. A useful paperwork process starts with that specific task: what information does it need, and why is that information unavailable?

<PostFourRevised />

The useful requirement is the information needed to finish a receiving task. Why it cannot be used determines whether a request, access change or correction would help.
